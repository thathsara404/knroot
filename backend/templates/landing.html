Features

Everything your curiosity
needs to take root

Real-world seeds, infinite branching, strength testing, and a community to grow alongside.

Seed from Real News

The AI curates the hottest stories across AI, biology, economics, geopolitics, health, and tech — refreshed daily. Pick one as your seed and real-world context makes the knowledge grow faster and stick longer.

Branch in Any Direction

Every concept can fork. Hit "Learn More" and the AI grows a new branch, going wider, deeper, or sideways into adjacent knowledge. No imposed structure. No ceiling.

Test Your Root Strength

AI-generated MCQ quizzes test your entire branch ancestry. Miss one? Relearn and retry. Pass? Branch even deeper from that exact node.

See Your Root System

Every session builds a live knowledge tree, with foundational concepts at the top, advanced branches below. Your entire learning journey visible at a glance, navigable at any node.

Community Root Garden

Earn Your Star Rating

How it works

From seed to root system
in four steps

Plant your first question. Then let it grow wherever your curiosity leads.

1

Choose your seed

Browse live news across 6 categories. Any article, from a quantum computing breakthrough to a central bank decision, can become the seed of a deep root system.

2

Grow your first root

Hit Explore. The AI extracts core concepts behind the story and maps them from foundational to advanced. Your first branch emerges instantly.

3

Branch wherever you're curious

Every concept has a "Learn More" button. Click it and the AI grows a new branch one level deeper. Repeat as many times as you like. There is no final node.

4

Test, share, and let others grow on yours

Run a knowledge check to stress-test what you've rooted. Share to the community wall. Watch others branch off your work.
