Mathemariza gives your curiosity everything it needs: real-world seeds, infinite branching, strength testing, and a community to grow alongside.

🌱

Seed from Real News

Every root system starts with a seed. Pick any live news story across AI, biology, economics, geopolitics, health, or tech. Plant it as your starting point. Real context makes knowledge grow faster and stick longer.

🌿

Branch in Any Direction

Like roots searching for nutrients, every concept can fork. Hit "Learn More" on any idea and the AI grows a new branch, going wider, deeper, or sideways into adjacent knowledge. No imposed structure. No ceiling.

🧠

Test Your Root Strength

Strong roots don't just grow, they hold. AI-generated MCQ quizzes test your entire branch ancestry. Miss one? Relearn and retry. Pass? Branch even deeper from that exact node.

🗺️

See Your Root System

Every session builds a live knowledge tree, a visual map of where your roots have spread. Foundational concepts at the top, advanced branches below. Your entire learning journey visible at a glance, navigable at any node.

🌳

Community Root Garden

Share your root system publicly. Others can explore your branches, import them into their own journey, add comments, and keep growing from where you left off. Roots shared are roots strengthened.

⭐

Earn Your Star Rating

Earn points when the community upvotes your shared roots. Reach 1,000 upvotes for your first star and climb to 10 million for the fifth. The deeper your roots grow, the stronger your reputation becomes.

Watch a root system
come to life

Four steps to plant your seed. Then it grows wherever your curiosity leads.

1

Choose your seed

Browse live news across 6 categories. Any article, from a quantum computing breakthrough to a central bank decision, can become the seed of a deep root system.

2

Grow your first root

Hit Explore. The AI extracts the core concepts behind the story and maps them from foundational to advanced. Your first branch emerges instantly.

3

Branch wherever you're curious

Every concept has a "Learn More" button. Click it and the AI grows a new branch one level deeper. Repeat as many times as you like. There is no final node.

4

Test, share, and let others grow on yours

Run a knowledge check to stress-test what you've rooted. Then share your root system to the community wall. Watch others branch off your work.
