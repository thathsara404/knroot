How scoring works

Votes on your shares

  • 👍 Upvote received +{{ scoring.upvote_pts }} pts
  • 👎 Downvote received {{ scoring.downvote_pts }} pts

Resharing others' posts

  • First time you reshare another user's post +{{ scoring.reshare_bonus_pts }} pts

Resharing the same post a second time earns no additional points. Resharing your own posts never earns a bonus.

Sharing low-quality content

  • Resharing a post that has {{ scoring.toxic_threshold_dv }}+ downvotes (checked at share time) {{ scoring.toxic_penalty_pts }} pts

Applies every time you share that post, including your own posts. Vote counts are snapshotted at the moment of sharing — future votes on the source post don't change what was already recorded.
